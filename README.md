# Patient Knowledge Graph Representation Using Non-Euclidean Metrics

Master’s thesis focused on the analysis of Machine Learning techniques applied to embeddings generated from non-Euclidean spaces, with the objective of improving diagnostic support for Mendelian diseases through graph-based patient representations.

The research investigates how the geometric structure of embedding spaces and the topology of biomedical knowledge graphs influence representation quality and downstream predictive performance.

---

# Research Objectives

The study is divided into three main analyses:

## 1. Representation Space Analysis
Evaluation of the impact of the embedding space geometry:
- Euclidean space
- Hyperbolic space

The goal is to understand how non-Euclidean geometries affect the representation of hierarchical biomedical relationships.

---

## 2. Graph Model Analysis
Comparison between different graph architectures:
- Homogeneous graph models
- Heterogeneous graph models

The analysis evaluates how graph structure influences learning capability and predictive accuracy.

---

## 3. Biomedical Consistency Analysis
Extension of the original graph through the integration of **Gene nodes** in order to evaluate:
- biomedical coherence of the graph
- biological consistency of learned representations
- impact on model performance
